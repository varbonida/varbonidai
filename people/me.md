## Voice & Style

Direct, plain, and short. Professional but not formal. States facts and next
steps without padding. Apologises once, briefly, and moves on. Quotes the other
person's line, then answers directly underneath it.

Avoid on his behalf: fluff, buzzwords, corporate or AI-sounding phrasing,
unnecessary explanation, restating the question before answering. Keep his
original meaning and wording where possible.

Samples (client messages, unedited):

> To clarify on what you want:
>  - A dictation app to be installed or built on your computer (not a web application)
>  - The app should be fully secure/private
>  - Highly accurate transcription
>
> For tomorrow, I will start by searching or finding any existing open source tools.

> Done some research on open source dictation apps and decided to move forward
> with VoiceInk. It runs on Mac and also has an iOS app, so it covers both
> devices. It's open source under GPL v3, which means I can fork it and build
> custom improvements over time (self-learning vocabulary, custom Modes, etc.)
> instead of being locked into their paid version.

> for the search i put in around 4hrs, including installing and testing both
> apps on mobile. I will be downloading the source codes for each ios & android.

Notes on what those show:

- Confirms scope back as a short bullet list before starting work.
- Gives the decision first, then the reasoning, in two or three sentences.
- Reports hours and concrete state plainly, no hedging or self-promotion.
- Common closings: "Noted on that", "Understood", "No problem, take your time".

## Working Preferences

- **Files at session end.** Durable knowledge gets filed at the end of a
  session without asking permission per edit. Anything genuinely ambiguous is
  flagged for a quick confirm rather than filed silently.
- **Never runs `git commit`.** The agent makes changes, summarises them, and
  suggests a commit message. Vergel reviews, stages, and commits himself. This
  has no exceptions.
- **No bank or payment credentials in the vault.** Invoicing facts (platform,
  periods, what was billed) are fine. Account numbers, card details, and
  payout credentials are not.
- Prefers the vault to stay simple: Obsidian, Markdown, Git, nothing else
  required.
