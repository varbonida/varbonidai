**Claude**

No payment needed — Q5 is asking about three things that are all free, they're just technical additions to the site's code, not paid services:

- **Open Graph tags** — these are just extra lines of HTML metadata (like `<meta property="og:image">`) that tell Pinterest, Facebook, etc. what image/title/description to show when someone shares your link. No cost, no signup, no third-party account — it's just code in the page itself.
- **Sitemap** — an auto-generated file (`sitemap.xml`) listing all your pages, which helps Google find and index them faster. Astro can generate this automatically at build time. No cost.
- **RSS feed** — a file (`feed.xml`) that lets people or services "subscribe" to new posts. Also auto-generated by Astro at build time, no cost, and honestly not critical for you — it's more useful for tech/news blogs than an affiliate site. You could even skip it without losing anything.

None of these involve a subscription, an account, or a fee. They're just code the agent writes once, and it's free forever since they're part of your own static site, not an external service.

Where costs *could* eventually show up in this whole project (none required to start):

- A custom domain later (~$10-15/year) — optional, the free `.vercel.app` URL works fine
- That's genuinely it for a static site like this on Vercel's free tier
