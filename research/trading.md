## Framework: "pre-LIT" Smart Money Concepts

Source: [[raw/research/trading/The pre LIT Basic Master Map.pdf|The pre LIT
Basic Master Map]], a course/mentor deck (branding suggests it is the
introductory map for a paid "LIT bootcamp"). This is the source's own framework,
presented here as a summary of what it teaches, not as verified trading advice.

The map has 11 numbered concepts, meant to be layered on top of each other:

1. **Supply and Demand** - price ranges build up orders; supply turns into
   demand and back again. These zones are Points of Interest (PoI) to watch for
   reversal or continuation.
2. **Imbalance** - also called inefficiency. The market "fairly delivers" price
   eventually, so it returns to fill zones where orders weren't fully matched.
   Used to place tight stops.
3. **Continuation & Reversal** - continuations are trend-like, reversals flip
   the trend. The source claims there are identifiable conditions for which one
   is happening, especially combined with imbalance.
4. **Multiple Timeframes (MTF)** - confirm trades across timeframes (e.g. H1 →
   M5 → M1) for higher-probability entries, better risk:reward, and tighter
   stops.
5. **Structure Basics** - distinguish main structure from internal structure.
   Internal structure sits inside a larger move and is fractal across
   timeframes; reading it helps anticipate the next likely move.
6. **Order-Flow Mitigation** - a trending market plus supply/demand plus
   imbalance mitigation. Small pullbacks grab liquidity before continuing,
   which traps trend traders.
7. **Continuation & Reversal (applied)** - Break of Structure (BoS) patterns
   read together with the above.
8. **Liquidity** - all the money moving through or resting in the market
   (stop losses, limit/stop orders). The source's framing: "if you can't see
   the liquidity, then you are the liquidity." Claimed to be only 5% of the
   course's actual teaching despite being the most visible concept.
9. **Advanced Structure** - BoS as a deliberate liquidity trap: a trend builds
   orders, breaks structure, catches both breakout and support/resistance
   traders on the wrong side, then reverses.
10. **Inducements** - price is induced to move one way to trigger retail
    orders before reversing. Continuation inducements make traders think price
    is reversing when it isn't; reversal inducements make traders believe a
    continuation when the market is about to reverse.
11. **Points of Interest (PoI), combined** - stacking inducement, imbalance,
    liquidity, and structure together into one entry setup.

The source is explicit that this is a marketing/curriculum map, not a full
manual - several sections say the real depth is "inside our LIT bootcamp." Its
own honesty check is notable: it claims 80% of Smart Money interest areas fail,
and that the concepts alone don't work without combining multiple confluences.

**This is one course's terminology and claims, not verified fact.** Standard
Smart Money Concepts / ICT vocabulary (supply/demand, BoS, liquidity,
inducement) is widely taught under many names; nothing here has been
cross-checked against outcomes or other sources.
