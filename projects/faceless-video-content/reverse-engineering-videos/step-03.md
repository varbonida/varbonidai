# Step 3 - Visual Filmmaking Analysis

Phase A. Continue the same Gemini conversation with Steps 1-2 available as
context. Save the result as `03-visual-filmmaking.md`. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]].

```
Reverse engineer the visual filmmaking of this short drama shot-by-shot where practical.

For each important shot or sequence, analyze:
- Timestamp
- Shot size
- Camera angle
- Camera movement
- Composition
- Subject positioning
- Lighting
- Color
- Background
- Props
- Facial expressions
- Body language
- Depth of field
- Visual emphasis
- What the camera wants the audience to notice

Do not merely describe what is visible.

Explain WHY each visual choice appears to have been made and what effect it has on the viewer.

Then identify recurring visual patterns:
- Shot selection during emotional moments
- Use of close-ups
- Use of wide shots
- Camera movement and tension
- Lighting and mood
- Color storytelling
- Visual foreshadowing
- Visual symbolism

Focus on the filmmaking decisions and their narrative purpose.
```
