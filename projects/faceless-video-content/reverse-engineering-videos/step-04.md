# Step 4 - Editing Strategy Analysis

Phase A. Continue the same Gemini conversation with Steps 1-3 available as
context. Save the result as `04-editing-strategy.md`. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]].

```
Reverse engineer the editing strategy of this short drama.

Analyze:
- Average shot duration
- Where cuts happen
- Why cuts happen
- Reaction shots
- Shot/reverse-shot
- Cross-cutting
- Jump cuts
- Match cuts
- Transitions
- Pacing
- Silence
- Music
- Sound effects

Identify the specific moments where editing increases:
- Curiosity
- Tension
- Emotional impact
- Surprise
- Urgency

Create a timeline showing how the pacing changes from the beginning to the end.

Explain the editing strategy in simple, practical terms so another filmmaker could reproduce it.

Focus on the underlying editing principles rather than merely describing the cuts.
```
