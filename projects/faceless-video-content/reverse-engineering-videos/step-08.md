# Step 8 - Production-Ready Recreation Plan

Phase B. Takes Steps 1-7 - the reference analysis plus the new story from
Step 7 - and turns it into a real shot list for the new video. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]].

```
Using the results from Steps 1-7, convert the story adaptation into a production-ready recreation plan.

For every scene and important shot, define:
- Shot number
- Scene number
- Timestamp
- Estimated duration
- Story purpose
- Characters present
- Character appearance/state
- Character action
- Facial expression
- Body language
- Environment
- Important props
- Composition
- Shot size
- Camera angle
- Camera movement
- Lighting
- Color palette
- Depth of field
- Atmosphere
- Audio/SFX
- Dialogue, if applicable
- Transition
- Emotional objective

The goal is to create a precise visual production plan that can later be converted into image and video generation prompts.

Maintain character, environment, spatial, lighting, and visual continuity between shots and scenes.
```
