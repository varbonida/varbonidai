# Step 1 - Story Structure Analysis

Phase A (reverse-engineer the reference). Run first, against the uploaded
reference video in Gemini. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]]
for how the six Phase A steps chain together and where to save the result
(`01-story-structure.md`).

```
Analyze this entire short drama from beginning to end.

Your first task is to reverse engineer the STORY STRUCTURE, not critique the video.

Create a chronological timeline with timestamps.

For each scene, identify:
- Start timestamp
- End timestamp
- Location
- Characters present
- What happens
- What information the audience learns
- What changes during the scene
- Emotional state of each important character
- Purpose of the scene
- How the scene creates a reason to continue watching

Then identify:
- Opening hook
- Protagonist
- Protagonist's initial situation
- Central conflict
- Inciting incident
- Major complications
- Midpoint or major revelation
- Escalation
- Climax
- Resolution
- Final emotional beat

Do not invent information that is not observable or reasonably inferred from the video.

Use timestamps throughout your analysis.
```
