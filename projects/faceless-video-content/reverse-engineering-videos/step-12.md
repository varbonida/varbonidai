# Step 12 - Shot-by-Shot Video Prompts

Phase B. Describes what changes from the Step 11 starting frame to the end
of the shot - motion only, not a restatement of the image prompt. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]].

```
Using the corresponding starting-frame image, Character Bible, Environment Bible, and shot plan, write the video-generation prompt for this shot.

Describe what happens FROM the starting frame TO the ending frame.

Include:
- Character movement
- Facial expression changes
- Body movement
- Interaction between characters
- Object movement
- Environmental movement
- Camera movement
- Camera speed
- Timing
- Emotional progression
- Important visual events
- Ending position/state

Preserve the character appearance, clothing, environment, lighting, composition, and atmosphere established in the starting frame.

Do not introduce unnecessary movements or new visual elements.

The video prompt should describe controlled cinematic motion rather than simply repeating the image description.
```
