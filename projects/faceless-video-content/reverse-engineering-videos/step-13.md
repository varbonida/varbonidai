# Step 13 - Generate, Edit, Compare, Iterate

Phase B, final step. Assembles the generated shots, checks the result
against the reverse-engineered structure from Phase A rather than just
against general quality, and loops back to fix specific shots rather than
regenerating everything. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]].

```
Use the completed production plan to generate the video shot-by-shot.

After generating the shots:
1. Assemble the shots in chronological order.
2. Match the planned pacing and shot durations.
3. Add dialogue, music, ambience, and sound effects.
4. Compare the result against the reverse-engineered structure.
5. Check story clarity.
6. Check character consistency.
7. Check environment continuity.
8. Check camera and composition consistency.
9. Check emotional progression.
10. Check pacing and editing rhythm.
11. Identify the weakest shots.
12. Revise their image or video prompts.
13. Regenerate the necessary shots.
14. Reassemble and review again.

Prioritize narrative clarity, visual continuity, emotional impact, and pacing.
```
