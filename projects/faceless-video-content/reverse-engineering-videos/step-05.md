# Step 5 - Dialogue Analysis

Phase A. Continue the same Gemini conversation with Steps 1-4 available as
context. Save the result as `05-dialogue.md`. See
[[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|the workflow overview]].

```
Analyze the dialogue in this short drama.

For each important exchange, identify:
- Timestamp
- Speaker
- Literal words
- Apparent meaning
- What the speaker wants from the other person
- Information revealed
- Information concealed
- Emotional subtext
- Power dynamic
- How the exchange changes the situation

Identify examples of:
- Exposition
- Conflict
- Subtext
- Foreshadowing
- Manipulation
- Emotional confrontation
- Revelations
- Callbacks
- Repeated phrases

Explain how the dialogue moves the story forward rather than functioning as simple narration.

Do not invent dialogue. If dialogue is unclear or inaudible, mark it as uncertain rather than guessing.
```
