# Faceless Video Content

Vergel's own project, not client work. Producing faceless, mostly AI-generated
video content (Facebook/YouTube Shorts and Reels) - a return to this line of
work.

## What is known

- Format: short-form vertical video (Shorts/Reels), mostly AI-generated,
  no on-camera presence ("faceless").
- Platforms: Facebook and YouTube.
- First concrete workstream: reverse-engineering short AI "drama" videos
  (the animal-drama style short vertical clips) to understand how they're
  built, then reusing the extracted structure to produce original videos -
  see [[projects/faceless-video-content/reverse-engineering-videos/index|Reverse-Engineering AI Drama Videos]].

## Source

Ingested from a ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Luna) conversation working out the
reverse-engineering methodology, filed at
[[raw/projects/faceless-video-content/Compare Gemini Claude Code.pdf|raw/projects/faceless-video-content/Compare Gemini Claude Code.pdf]].
The conversation iterated through several discarded, more elaborate versions
of the workflow before settling on the canonical one recorded here - only the
final, agreed-on version was kept; the abandoned drafts were not.
