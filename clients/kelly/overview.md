## Relationship & Working Style

Things learned from working with her so far. These are durable and should shape
how work is communicated:

- **Call, do not announce a call.** She said this directly on 2026-08-17: "I
  don't need emails telling me you're going to call. Just pick up the phone and
  call." Voice is her preferred channel, text is for artefacts and links.
- **Ask early, do not guess.** On 2026-08-20: "If something is unclear, please
  ask. I would rather fix it early than have you guessing or feeling uncertain."
- **She travels constantly.** Motorhome, moving between Germany and elsewhere in
  Europe, with real Schengen-zone deadline pressure. Expect stretches of no
  contact caused by breakdowns, dead power, and bad internet, not disinterest.
- **Apple ecosystem.** Mac and iPhone. Any deliverable needs to work there.
- **She briefs by dictation.** Requirements arrive as loose spoken lists,
  sometimes garbled. Confirming scope back as a short bullet list before
  starting has worked well.
- **She sets focus explicitly.** On 2026-08-20 she narrowed scope to the
  dictation app only, and parked the rest of her list.
- She sees **Never Pay Double** as the project that will actually make money;
  the dictation app was chosen first deliberately as a way to learn to work
  together.
