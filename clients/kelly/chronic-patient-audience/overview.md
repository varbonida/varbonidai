# Chronic Patient Audience

Client: [[clients/kelly/overview|Kelly]].

Audience research. Not started.

## What is known

From her 2026-08-10 project list, verbatim: "Best way to tap into 'chronic
patient' groups."

Her own quotation marks around "chronic patient". This is the only item on her
list that points at a specific audience, and it lines up with her Dr Citizen
brand and the simplevitality address, which both read as health-adjacent.

## Handle with care

Chronic illness communities are, by definition, vulnerable people, and they are
heavily targeted by health marketing already. Patient support groups also tend
to have explicit rules against commercial outreach, and moderators enforce them.
Anything built here should be worth their attention on its own merits. Getting
this wrong damages the brand it is meant to help.

If any of it involves collecting personal information from people in the EU,
health data is a special category under GDPR with a much higher bar than
ordinary contact data.
