## Rules for drafting on his behalf

Learned from how he edited what was drafted for him:

- **Never inflate experience.** A generated cover letter claimed ten years of
  experience and he had it removed. Do not put a duration, a seniority level, or
  a claim into his materials that he has not confirmed.
- Short and direct. He repeatedly asked for things to be shortened and
  humanised after they came back inflated.
- No em-dashes. He asked for them stripped explicitly.
- Do not oversell gaps in the stack. He questioned whether hedging lines like
  "I don't tick every box but I pick things up fast" were even necessary.
